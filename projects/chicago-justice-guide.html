About

Chicago Justice Guide

This public-information project was developed to help Chicago residents better understand common legal situations before seeking professional legal assistance. It organizes information from trusted government agencies, courts, and nonprofit organizations into plain language and practical referral pathways.

What this guide can help you do

  • Understand common legal issues before seeking assistance.
  • Identify which court, agency, or organization generally handles an issue.
  • Recognize common legal terminology.
  • Prepare basic questions and documents before meeting an attorney or advocate.
  • Locate trusted public resources.

Important disclaimer

This website provides general legal information and links to public resources. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Laws, procedures, contact information, and eligibility requirements can change. Verify important deadlines and requirements with the linked official source or a qualified legal professional.
